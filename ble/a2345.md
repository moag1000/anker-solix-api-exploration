# A2345 (250 W Prime Charger) BLE

The 250 W Prime Charger (A2345). This page owns the BLE-specific facts of the product; the command payloads (opcodes, TLV tags, scales) are owned by the MQTT page [../mqtt/a2345.md](../mqtt/a2345.md) and are not repeated here. The frame, fragmentation and encryption are on [protocol.md](protocol.md), the negotiation on [handshake.md](handshake.md).

Evidence is the asm of 3.24.3 (`file.dart @0xADDR`, relative to `charging/ble/device/` unless a path is given). Pool lists such as `[0, 0x5e]` are real values. A row that says `no` in the column `on the MQTT page` means the opcode is not mentioned on the linked page; it can still be a command that also goes over MQTT.

Notes on this product:

- `A2345AnkerDevice` mixes in `ZXBleFeature`, `A2345DeviceFeature` and `BleOtaField`; `A2345DeviceController` additionally mixes in `PlatformCustomProtocolHttpMixin`, a mixin whose name points to HTTP calls for custom charging protocols (its endpoints were not traced for this page). It references the `02xx` and `03xx` opcode ranges and the `0a00` to `0a23` responses; the A91B2 controller shares most of them.
- The 3.24.3 controller references `021d` and `0a1d`, which 3.18.0 did not.
- Negotiation, app side: `A2345AnkerDevice` (class id 8470) has `ZXBleFeature` in its `extends` line and `A2345` is not a registered SDK product code, so `BleHandler` picks `BleHandlerImpl1`, whose `_authDevice` starts the Dart `ZXBleConfer`. Which of the three conferers then runs depends on the capability response, not on the product ([handshake.md](handshake.md#which-implementation-runs-for-a-product)).

#### Direction

both: requests go app to device, the responses (`08xx` in the common map, [handshake.md](handshake.md#opcode-pairs)) come back device to app.

#### Packet framing

[protocol.md](protocol.md#frame-on-ble); the handshake frames carry the flag bits of [protocol.md](protocol.md#opcode-byte-0-flags).

#### Handshake prerequisite

The rows of group `handshake` are the handshake. Which conferer runs (`SecureEcdhConfer`, `EcdhConfer` or `AesConferV1`) is chosen from the capability response, not from the product: [handshake.md](handshake.md#capability-negotiation-response). The other rows are ordinary commands that need a completed handshake for encrypted frames.

#### TLV tags

The handshake payloads are positional bytes or single tags and are not per-product; they are described in [handshake.md](handshake.md#capability-negotiation-response) and [handshake.md](handshake.md#the-three-conferers). The payloads of the WiFi, OTA and status-request rows were not decoded for this page.

#### Example

#### Direction

app to device for the requests; the matching responses and the status messages `0a00` and `0303` (app side: the controller binds these two ids to its parsers; there is no `0405` compare, see [the MQTT page](../mqtt/a2345.md#parsedeviceallinfo-status-tags-upstream-message-0a00); the opcode map is [protocol.md](protocol.md#zx-opcode-names)) come back device to app.

#### Packet framing

[protocol.md](protocol.md#frame-on-ble) and [protocol.md](protocol.md#fragmentation); the controller's `sendCommand` waits 5 s by default ([protocol.md](protocol.md#timeouts-and-limits)).

#### Handshake prerequisite

A completed handshake is needed for encrypted commands: the frame builder encrypts only when the device has a key ([protocol.md](protocol.md#payload-encryption)).

#### TLV tags

Owned by the linked MQTT page: [../mqtt/a2345.md](../mqtt/a2345.md). Opcodes marked `no` in the table have no tag table there. Their payload shapes, read from the controller methods (shape definitions: [protocol.md](protocol.md#controller-send-wrappers)): `getOTAInfo` and `sendSubPackageData` (shape OTA info and OTA data); `getOTAState` (shape OTA state). Response handling: none in these methods, see the end of [Controller send wrappers](protocol.md#controller-send-wrappers).

none: the MQTT pages hold the synthesized frames of their products; this page adds none.

#### Known unknowns

- Rows marked `no` may be commands that are MQTT-only on the cloud side or simply undocumented on the MQTT page; this page does not decide which.
- The payload tags and response handling of the 61 rows marked `no` that follow no shape of [protocol.md](protocol.md#controller-send-wrappers). — traced 2026-10-20: by the call content of each function digest (`a2345_device_controller.dart`): 37 contain a `Map._fromLiteral` and no send call, 21 contain a map and a send call (4 of them also `intToList`), 3 send without a map; the names include `_handleResponse` and `bindSuccess`, which are not send methods; the maps of the setters (for example `setChargingMode`, `setDCPortTimedTask`, `setClockDisplayTime`) were not decoded key by key.
