# A17A3, A17A4, A17A5 (SOLIX Everfrost 2) BLE

The SOLIX Everfrost 2 coolers (26 L, 40 L and 58 L). This page owns the BLE-specific facts of the product; the command payloads (opcodes, TLV tags, scales) are owned by no MQTT page (the product is not covered by [../mqtt/README.md](../mqtt/README.md)); the BLE opcode lists below are the only command facts this repository has for it. The frame, fragmentation and encryption are on [protocol.md](protocol.md), the negotiation on [handshake.md](handshake.md).

Evidence is the asm of 3.24.3 (`file.dart @0xADDR`, relative to `charging/ble/device/` unless a path is given). Pool lists such as `[0, 0x5e]` are real values. A row that says `no` in the column `on the MQTT page` means the opcode is not mentioned on the linked page; it can still be a command that also goes over MQTT.

Notes on this product:

- `A17A3AnkerDevice` mixes in `ZXBleFeature`, `ZXCoolerDeviceFeature` and `BleOtaField`; `A17A4AnkerDevice` and `A17A5AnkerDevice` extend it. The factory gives A17A4 and A17A5 the `A17A3DeviceController` (`device_factory_impl.dart @0x240e514`, `@0x240e570`), and A17A1 and A17A2 the `A17A0DeviceController`. The ProductModels use the ZX GATT triple.
- The files are shared with [a17a0.md](a17a0.md): `a17a3_anker_device.dart`, `a17a3_device_controller.dart` and `zx_cooler_device_feature.dart` (class `BatteryParams` and `ZXCoolerDeviceFeature`).
- There is no MQTT page for the coolers.
- Negotiation, app side: `A17A3AnkerDevice` and its subclasses (class ids 8475 to 8477) has `ZXBleFeature` in its `extends` line and `A17A3`, `A17A4` and `A17A5` is not a registered SDK product code, so `BleHandler` picks `BleHandlerImpl1`, whose `_authDevice` starts the Dart `ZXBleConfer`. Which of the three conferers then runs depends on the capability response, not on the product ([handshake.md](handshake.md#which-implementation-runs-for-a-product)).

#### Direction

both: requests go app to device, the responses (`08xx` in the common map, [handshake.md](handshake.md#opcode-pairs)) come back device to app.

#### Packet framing

[protocol.md](protocol.md#frame-on-ble); the handshake frames carry the flag bits of [protocol.md](protocol.md#opcode-byte-0-flags).

#### Handshake prerequisite

The rows of group `handshake` are the handshake. Which conferer runs (`SecureEcdhConfer`, `EcdhConfer` or `AesConferV1`) is chosen from the capability response, not from the product: [handshake.md](handshake.md#capability-negotiation-response). The other rows are ordinary commands that need a completed handshake for encrypted frames.

#### TLV tags

The handshake payloads are positional bytes or single tags and are not per-product; they are described in [handshake.md](handshake.md#capability-negotiation-response) and [handshake.md](handshake.md#the-three-conferers). The payloads of the WiFi, OTA and status-request rows were not decoded for this page.

none: no capture of a handshake exists in this repository.

#### Related API endpoint

none: the handshake has no MQTT twin and the product has no MQTT page.

#### Direction

app to device for the requests; the matching responses and the periodic status push `0405` (`postDeviceAllInfoReq` in the charging map, [protocol.md](protocol.md#zx-opcode-names)) come back device to app.

#### Packet framing

[protocol.md](protocol.md#frame-on-ble) and [protocol.md](protocol.md#fragmentation); the controller's `sendCommand` waits 5 s by default ([protocol.md](protocol.md#timeouts-and-limits)).

#### Handshake prerequisite

A completed handshake is needed for encrypted commands: the frame builder encrypts only when the device has a key ([protocol.md](protocol.md#payload-encryption)).

#### TLV tags

There is no MQTT page. Payload shapes of the rows, read from the controller methods (shape definitions: [protocol.md](protocol.md#controller-send-wrappers)): `getOTAInfo` (`002e` or `0063`) and `sendSubPackageData` (`002f` or `0064`) take the opcode pair from `isMTU` and send the caller's map; `getOTAState` (`0030`) sends tag 0xA2 with a user-model string; `setDeviceUpdateEnergy` (`0057`) sends tags 0xA2 and 0xA3; one-byte setters: `setTemperatureUnit` (`0050`), `setVoltageProtection` (`0083`), `setScreenAlwaysOn` (`0082`), `setLcdBrightness` (`004c`), `setBox0Mode` (`0081`); requests without payload: `getUpgradeCondition` (`0089`), `setReset` (`0051`); `getAllDeviceInfo` (`0040`, `a17a3_device_controller.dart @0x242c10c`) sends an empty payload and then calls `getOTAState` and `getUpgradeCondition` (`@0x242c120`, `@0x242c128`); `boxParamsFn` (`0080`, `@0x30491b0`) sends an empty payload with the named `valueMap` set to its argument. The response of a request is not handled in these methods (they return Null after the send).
