# A1771 (F1200 Bluetooth and WLAN) BLE

The F1200 with Bluetooth and WLAN (A1771). It is the base class of a large family. This page owns the BLE-specific facts of the product; the command payloads (opcodes, TLV tags, scales) are owned by the MQTT page [../mqtt/a1771.md](../mqtt/a1771.md) and are not repeated here. The frame, fragmentation and encryption are on [protocol.md](protocol.md), the negotiation on [handshake.md](handshake.md).

Evidence is the asm of 3.24.3 (`file.dart @0xADDR`, relative to `charging/ble/device/` unless a path is given). Pool lists such as `[0, 0x5e]` are real values. A row that says `no` in the column `on the MQTT page` means the opcode is not mentioned on the linked page; it can still be a command that also goes over MQTT.

Notes on this product:

- `A1771AnkerDevice` is the root of the family: `A1772AnkerDevice`, `A1781AnkerDevice`, `A1753AnkerDevice` (and its subclasses) and `A1790AnkerDevice` extend it, and the matching controller and command classes extend `A1771DeviceController` and `A1771DeviceCommands`. The product has its own GATT family (`F1200 (2540)` in the table of [protocol.md](protocol.md#gatt-profile-per-product)).
- `A1771DeviceController` mixes in `PpsDeviceFeature` and `DeviceLogFeature`; the opcodes the class itself references are in the controller table below.
- Negotiation, app side: `A1771AnkerDevice` (class id 8483) has `ZXBleFeature` in its `extends` line and `A1771` is not a registered SDK product code, so `BleHandler` picks `BleHandlerImpl1`, whose `_authDevice` starts the Dart `ZXBleConfer`. Which of the three conferers then runs depends on the capability response, not on the product ([handshake.md](handshake.md#which-implementation-runs-for-a-product)).

#### Direction

both: requests go app to device, the responses (`08xx` in the common map, [handshake.md](handshake.md#opcode-pairs)) come back device to app.

#### Packet framing

[protocol.md](protocol.md#frame-on-ble); the handshake frames carry the flag bits of [protocol.md](protocol.md#opcode-byte-0-flags).

#### Handshake prerequisite

The rows of group `handshake` are the handshake. Which conferer runs (`SecureEcdhConfer`, `EcdhConfer` or `AesConferV1`) is chosen from the capability response, not from the product: [handshake.md](handshake.md#capability-negotiation-response). The other rows are ordinary commands that need a completed handshake for encrypted frames.

#### TLV tags

The handshake payloads are positional bytes or single tags and are not per-product; they are described in [handshake.md](handshake.md#capability-negotiation-response) and [handshake.md](handshake.md#the-three-conferers). The payloads of the WiFi, OTA and status-request rows were not decoded for this page.

#### Direction

app to device for the requests; the matching responses and the periodic status push `0405` (`postDeviceAllInfoReq` in the charging map, [protocol.md](protocol.md#zx-opcode-names)) come back device to app.

#### Packet framing

[protocol.md](protocol.md#frame-on-ble) and [protocol.md](protocol.md#fragmentation); the controller's `sendCommand` waits 5 s by default ([protocol.md](protocol.md#timeouts-and-limits)).

#### Handshake prerequisite

A completed handshake is needed for encrypted commands: the frame builder encrypts only when the device has a key ([protocol.md](protocol.md#payload-encryption)).

#### TLV tags

Owned by the linked MQTT page: [../mqtt/a1771.md](../mqtt/a1771.md). Opcodes marked `no` in the table have no tag table there. Their payload shapes, read from the controller methods (shape definitions: [protocol.md](protocol.md#controller-send-wrappers)): `setDeviceUpdateEnergy` (shape update energy); one-byte setters (shape one-byte setter): `setAcOutputSmartMode` (`0077`), `setCarPortSavingMode` (`0076`); `setSuperCharge` (`005e`) sends `[1]` or `[0]` by its boolean argument (`a1771_device_controller.dart @0x3c53c6c`); `setMajorPkg` (`0048`) sends the two arguments as `[a, b]` (`@0x264df24`); `getAllDeviceInfo` (`0040`, `a1771_device_controller.dart @0x40f4848`) is a status request (shape status request). Response handling: none in these methods, see the end of [Controller send wrappers](protocol.md#controller-send-wrappers).
