# A17E2, AE103, AE111, AE113, A17E1 (Solarbank E2 series and E10) BLE

The E2 series (A17E2, AE103, AE111, AE113; `DeviceConstant.isE2Series`) and the E10 (A17E1). A17E2, AE103, AE111 and AE113 are new in 3.24.3 as `ProductModel` entries (A17E1 existed). There is no `charging/ble/device/` directory for any of them. This page owns the BLE-specific facts of the product; the command payloads (opcodes, TLV tags, scales) are owned by no MQTT page (the product is not covered by [../mqtt/README.md](../mqtt/README.md)); the BLE opcode lists below are the only command facts this repository has for it. The frame, fragmentation and encryption are on [protocol.md](protocol.md), the negotiation on [handshake.md](handshake.md).

Evidence is the asm of 3.24.3 (`file.dart @0xADDR`, relative to `charging/ble/device/` unless a path is given). Pool lists such as `[0, 0x5e]` are real values. A row that says `no` in the column `on the MQTT page` means the opcode is not mentioned on the linked page; it can still be a command that also goes over MQTT.

Notes on this product:

- `DeviceFactoryImpl.createByProductCode` has no branch for these codes, so the generic device classes of `charging/ble/device` do not apply; the app handles them in the `micro_hes` module (`micro_hes/src/a17e2`, `micro_hes/adapter/a17e2_device_info_adapter.dart`, `micro_hes/factory/a17e2_device_info_factory.dart`) and in `ak_soft_ems/util/a17e2_command.dart`. How BLE is used for them was not traced.
- All five models are in the product-scan map and use the ZX UUID triple (table in [protocol.md](protocol.md#gatt-profile-per-product)). `isE2Series` also drives `needsDefaultParameters` and `supportsSellElectricity` (`device_constant.dart @0x3515db0`, `@0x37a0054`).
